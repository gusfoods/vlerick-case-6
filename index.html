Beyond the fridge.

Fruit, soup, coffee, snacks and a pantry with no fresh food at all. One sixth of our revenue, and the part of Gus nobody has ever designed. Two people, one day.

Andreas De Ridder · Co-founder, Gus Foods  ·  hosted by Prof. Koen Tackx, Vlerick Business School

The company

A smart fridge full of chef-quality local meals — in your office.

Gus Foods builds its own connected fridges and stocks them with fresh, locally sourced meals. Employees scan a QR code, grab lunch, pay in the app — 24/7. One square metre, one power socket. Our vans restock each site up to three times a week. Founded in October 2020 by two ex-Delhaize colleagues; a core team of six.

The white space

The van already stops there. We just never ask.

  • 50 fridge customers buy nothing beyond the fridge — although we are at their door up to three times a week
  • About 23 customers have no fridge at all and buy fruit or soup only: 20% of Room Service revenue
  • Snacks and drinks: €135k a year inside our fridges, and essentially zero through Room Service
  • A fridge customer who does buy spends a median €4,500 a year on top of the fridge
  • We do not know why the other two thirds never bought: never asked, other supplier, no budget, no interest?
  • The offer is broad. Seven product families and a catalogue. It has to become as simple as possible for the customer
Two tests running now

What if there is no fresh food at all? The pantry.

Pilot A · The managed cupboard

  • A software company. No fridge, no app, no fee
  • We fill their own kitchen cupboards with snacks, drinks and breakfast items every two weeks
  • The employer pays for what is delivered; free for employees
  • First result: over €2,000 invoiced for October, similar expected for November

Pilot B · The long-life fridge

  • A normal Gus fridge, stocked only with items that keep at least two weeks
  • One delivery every two weeks instead of up to three a week
  • Employer pays about €500/month instead of €850
  • Employees pay per item in the app, as at any Gus fridge · no results yet

Both are young and small. Nobody has decided whether either is a product, an upsell, an entry offer for smaller offices, or a distraction.

Working with AI · Vlerick guidelines

Human in the Lead — assisted by AI.

You own every decision, framework choice and recommendation. AI accelerates — it never chooses. Never one mega-prompt: three staged passes with your judgment between each. With one day and two people, that discipline is what makes six tasks possible.

1 · Analysisload the package first · name the Value Pyramid · ask for tables you can check row by row
2 · Synthesisfeed verified results back in · ask for options, not a pick · you choose angle and format
3 · Recommendationname the asset · every claim traces to a proof · rewrite in your own voice
